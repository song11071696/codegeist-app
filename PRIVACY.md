# Release Notes Copilot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Release Notes Copilot is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence built by If Ty (individual developer).

## What data the app accesses
When you use it, the app reads Jira issues (summary, type, status, labels, fix version, assignee name, issue key) for the release or JQL you choose, and lists Confluence spaces you can see. When you click Publish, it creates a Confluence page in the space you pick.

## What data the app stores
None. The app has no database or Forge storage. It does not send data to any service outside Atlassian, and it does not use analytics, tracking or third-party AI services. All processing runs on Atlassian Forge infrastructure.

## Personal data
The app only handles assignee display names already visible to you in Jira, and only to show them in the release notes you generate. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

## Data retention and deletion
Nothing is retained by the app. Pages it publishes belong to your Confluence site and can be edited or deleted like any other page. Uninstalling the app removes all of its access.
